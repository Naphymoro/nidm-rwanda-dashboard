Publication planning

Turn NDIM into a clean, honest manuscript plan.

This workspace helps you prepare a publication-quality draft without pretending that the method is already fully validated. It keeps the story simple: what NDIM is, why it differs from ordinary misinformation-contagion models, what has been implemented, what still needs evidence, and which claims are safe to make at alpha stage.

Format guidance is based on Frontiers author guidance, Frontiers article-type guidance, and the Frontiers in Human Dynamics journal sections. Always confirm the final manuscript against the current journal website before submission.

Manuscript basics

Core manuscript text

Manuscript outline
  1. Problem: clean-cooking adoption is shaped by stories, trust, fear, cost, identity, and social influence.
  2. Gap: many contagion models describe spread, but do not preserve governed narrative evidence through to policy output.
  3. Innovation: NDIM links narrative ingestion, SDMX-style governance, inoculation-aware encoding, simulation, uncertainty, and policy synthesis.
  4. Method: describe evidence routes, ledger, encoding variables, compartmental model, agent model, digital twin, Bayesian update, RL optimizer, and policy brief.
  5. Alpha evidence: report software stress tests and synthetic narratives as engineering validation only.
  6. Future validation: double coding, inter-rater reliability, LLM-human agreement, field outcomes, and sensitivity analysis.
