Scientific publication

Prepare the argument before claiming the finding.

This page frames NDIM as a methods and software contribution. The current status should be described carefully: conceptually developed, implemented as an alpha tool, and awaiting field validation with double-coded data.

Suggested article shape

  1. Problem: policy teams need to use narrative evidence without losing context.
  2. Gap: diffusion models often simplify meaning too early.
  3. Method: NDIM links governed narratives, inoculation-aware encoding, simulation, uncertainty, and policy output.
  4. Validation plan: double coding, inter-rater reliability, LLM-human agreement, and field testing.

Claim boundary

Until field validation is complete, NDIM should not be presented as a proven predictor. It should be presented as a research workflow and computational framework ready for empirical testing.
