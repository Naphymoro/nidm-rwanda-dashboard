Export behavior

The primary exports are `.tex`, `.bib`, `.html`, RIS, CSV, and browser print-to-PDF. JSON-style audit export remains in the NDIM tool, but this page focuses on human-readable manuscript preparation.

Privacy rule

The publication workspace keeps drafts in your browser local storage. OpenAI calls require your explicit click and your key. Literature search only crawls legal metadata/open sources.
