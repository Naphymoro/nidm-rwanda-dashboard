HOW TO USE THIS SPACE
From interview material to a defensible research question
Start here
NIDM separates what people said from what you want to investigate. It then creates an inspectable plan, runs a bounded scientific tool, and preserves the assumptions and limitations alongside the result.
1. Evidence
Paste a clearly labelled interview excerpt or attach a text file. Evidence is the source material, not an instruction to the system.
2. Interpretation
Encoding identifies language signals such as trust, barriers or adoption themes. These are heuristic cues for review, not facts about a population.
3. Scenario
A scenario changes one explicit intervention setting while keeping the comparison inputs aligned. It illustrates possible dynamics; it is not a forecast.
