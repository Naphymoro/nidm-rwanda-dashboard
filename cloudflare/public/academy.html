Learning path

Learn the model before trusting the output.

NDIM is needed because adoption decisions are shaped by stories, trust, social influence, memory, fear, identity, and evidence. A spreadsheet of survey counts alone cannot show that chain.

Why narratives matter

A narrative is not just text. It is a situated account of experience. It carries who spoke, where the story was heard, what people fear, who they trust, and what future they imagine.

What inoculation means

Inoculation theory asks whether people can be prepared before harmful misinformation spreads. A weak warning plus a clear refutation can build resistance, much like a social vaccine.

What makes NDIM different

Many misinformation models begin after stories have already been simplified into states. NDIM begins earlier: with governed evidence, meaning, trust, place, and human review.
