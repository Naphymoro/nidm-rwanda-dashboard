Manual

How to use NDIM without getting lost.

Start with evidence. Do not start with the model. NDIM treats the model as advisory until narratives have source, place, consent, review status, and enough context to be interpreted responsibly.

1. Create or open a workspace

A workspace keeps one project separate from another. For example, ClimateTales Rwanda should live in its own workspace instead of being mixed with a generic clean-cooking pilot.

2. Ingest narratives

Choose the evidence route, load or paste the story, then add country, administrative unit, language, source, period, consent, and visibility.

3. Review and commit evidence

Approved records enter the accepted repository. Rejected records remain traceable but should not drive policy claims.

4. Encode meaning

Manual, AI, and hybrid encoding should be compared. Scores should be understandable and attached back to the narrative record.

5. Model and interpret

Compartmental and agent-based models help explore what may happen under assumptions. They are not proof by themselves.

6. Export a policy brief

The final output should be a readable decision brief with confidence level, evidence grade, assumptions, limitations, and required human review.
